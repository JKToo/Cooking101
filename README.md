### Cooking 101  
All-in-one kitchen companion, designed to make cooking easier and more enjoyable. Keep track of all your favorite recipes in one place, and effortlessly search for new ones based on the ingredients you have or the type of dish you’re craving. With built-in cooking tools like a timer and unit converter, you’ll never have to fumble with multiple apps while preparing meals. Whether you’re a beginner or a seasoned chef, Cooking 101 helps streamline your cooking process, so you can focus on creating delicious dishes with confidence.

### Key Features
✅ Recipe Manager – Save, organize, and access all your favorite recipes in one convenient place.

✅ Smart Recipe Search – Find new recipes based on the ingredients you have or by specifying the type of dish you want to make.

✅ Built-in Cooking Timer – Set timers directly in the app to keep track of cooking times without needing a separate timer.

✅ Unit Converter – Easily convert measurements between different units (e.g., cups to grams, Fahrenheit to Celsius) for hassle-free cooking.

✅ Step-by-Step Instructions – Get clear, easy-to-follow instructions for each recipe, ensuring perfect results every time.

✅ Favorites & Bookmarks – Quickly save your go-to recipes for easy access later.

✅ Ingredient Substitutions – Find alternative ingredients when you’re missing something from a recipe.
